# The Hired Wall — Community Success Ledger

Landed an interview pipeline, freelance contract, or full-time engineering offer using **`job-hunter-os`**? Add your story via Pull Request or GitHub Issue so builders everywhere can learn what workflows, regional boards, and CV strategies worked!

> Inspired by `career-ops-hq/career-ops`'s `HIRED.md` community ledger: entries are permanent and numbered sequentially, real names are always optional, and **zero private resumes, tokens, or employer correspondence should ever be submitted**.

---

## How to Add Your Entry (2-Minute PR)

1. Fork `redasalem/job-hunter-os` and append a new block directly under `<!-- ENTRIES -->` below (incrementing `Hire #N` from the previous top entry).
2. Choose your privacy tier:
   - **Public Handle:** Include your GitHub `@handle`, avatar/profile link, target role, sector, region, weeks elapsed, and 1–2 practical tips.
   - **Role-Only Anonymous:** Omit your GitHub handle and list only role, region, weeks elapsed, and setup tips.
3. Run the PII check (`skills/14-privacy-pii-shield.md` mindset: no emails, phone numbers, compensation leaks, or real `context/` files) and open a Pull Request!
